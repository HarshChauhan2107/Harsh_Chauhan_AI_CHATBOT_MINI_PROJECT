## Accuracy notes

1. The MCA page states a two-year/four-semester standard programme and also describes eligibility information for a one-year route for certain four-year graduates. The chatbot advises applicants to obtain written confirmation of the route that applies to their qualification.
2. The MCA fee response uses the MCA entry displayed in the official fee table at the time of review. Fees must be reconfirmed for the student's session, specialisation and category before payment.
3. Scholarship percentages can vary by programme/session; current policy pages should be checked individually. The chatbot avoids promising a scholarship amount based only on a generic marks range.
4. Admission application fee and number of choices are copied as displayed on the admission-process page at review time and may be revised by a newer portal notice.
5. AURA has no live connection to ERP, examination results, application records, hostel inventory, payments or transport seat allocation.
